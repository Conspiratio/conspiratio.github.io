Conspiratio is a free, turn-based economic simulation set in the early modern period,
inspired by *Die Fugger II*. You inherit a run-down workshop, build a trading business across
fourteen cities, climb from burgher to duke through titles and public office, and scheme
against your rivals — all while making sure your dynasty outlives you.

The game is **German-only**. There is no English translation yet, and producing one is one of
the ways you could help.
